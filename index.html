What a show is like

Kids are part of the act
Volunteers come up to help with the tricks, and Dana tailors the show to the ages in the room, and sometimes to the season.
It's funny
Parents bring up his sense of humor as often as the tricks. One wrote that he "had some great jokes for the grownups that went right over the Littles heads."
About an hour, start to finish
Long enough to be the main event of the party, and paced so that even five-year-olds stay with him the whole way.
Small rooms and big crowds
He has played to seven kids in a living room and to well over a hundred at a community Christmas party.
Schools and community events
Besides birthdays, Dana performs school assemblies, after-school programs, neighborhood events and shows for senior communities.
Close-up magic for children and adults

Where Dana performs

Dana lives in Amherst, near Manchester, and brings his table and props to you: living rooms, day cares, schools, function halls and community centers.

Recent shows have taken him to Nashua, Merrimack, Litchfield and Derry, north to Hopkinton, Gilmanton and Claremont, and over the state line to Lowell, Chelmsford and Tewksbury in Massachusetts.

He travels up to about 50 miles from Amherst. Not sure whether that covers your town? Ask when you check your date.

About Dana

Dana bought his first trick at Al Cohen's magic shop in Washington, DC, and he was hooked. Since then he has performed around Washington, DC, in West Virginia, and now in New Hampshire.

Mostly that has meant birthday parties. It has also meant schools, senior homes, a McDonald's, a Halloween festival in a park, and close-up magic at many restaurants. He is a member of Ring 166 of the International Brotherhood of Magicians, and you can watch some of his close-up routines on the Videos page.

Questions parents ask

How long is the show?

About an hour. One parent called it "the perfect length of time."

What ages is it for?

Dana adjusts the show to the ages in the room. Parents have booked him for groups from toddlers to 12-year-olds, and the grown-ups enjoy it too.

Do you come to our house?

Yes. Dana brings his own table and props, and fits into living rooms, day cares, classrooms and function halls.

Is the rabbit real?

Yes. A live rabbit appears from his top hat at the beginning of every stage show, and after the show everyone, kids and grown-ups alike, gets to pet the rabbit.

Do you perform for schools and groups?

Yes: school assemblies, after-school programs, neighborhood and community events, fundraisers and senior communities.

Do you do close-up magic?

Yes, for children and adults. Dana performs sleight-of-hand magic up close at adult parties, Halloween parties and events, moving table to table or standing in one place.

How far do you travel?

Up to about 50 miles from Amherst, which covers southern New Hampshire and nearby Massachusetts. Tell him your town when you check your date.

How much does a show cost?

Shows start at $150. Check your date and Dana will give you a price for your party.
